<!-- The instruction of the tile « Analyser ce que je vois » (spec 3.0 section 3.4, practice 3) -->

The person wants an **analysis of what they see**: the map open, or the steps selected when there are some. Reply with
`findings`, the most costly first: waits and waiting times, re-entries and copy and paste, repeated tasks, steps without
an actor or a sheet, loops, breaks in the flow (a step without incoming or outgoing flow, a decision with one branch),
documents often incomplete (low %C&A) and the hours they lose. Each finding names its objects by their codes and gives a
concrete suggestion. Weigh them with the data of the sheets (frequency, duration, hours lost per month); say when the
sheets are too empty to weigh. When the person asks for next steps or first drafts (sheets of the selected steps,
missing steps), and the exchange allows a proposal, give a `proposal` of the wanted state with the drafts.
