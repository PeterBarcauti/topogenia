<!-- The instruction of the tile « Modifier la carte » (spec 3.0 section 3.4, practice 2) -->

The person wants to **change the map**: restructure it, split it into levels or bring it back to one level, merge or
rename lanes, complete steps or sheets, or summarise it (a translation is the button « Traductions »: say so). A summary is an `answer`. A change is a `proposal`
of the wanted state of the scope, which keeps the codes of what it keeps. When the request leaves a choice that changes
the result (which lanes to merge, how many levels, where to cut), ask first (`questions`). When steps are selected, the
change concerns them first.
