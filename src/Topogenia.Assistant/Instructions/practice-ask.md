<!-- The instruction of the tile « Poser une question » (spec 3.0 section 3.4, practice 4) -->

The person asks a **question** about the process. Answer it (`answer`) from the data of the project, the schema and the
map open: which steps use a document, who does what, how long, how often, how many hours are lost and where. Give the
figure and how you got it in one line; cite the codes. When the data do not hold the answer, say what is missing. Change
nothing.
